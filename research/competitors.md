## Bottom line for the matrix
- The two slices of our idea that look newest, "answer leakage" and "mutation testing of graders", both already have open-source tools (BenchJack/BenchProbe; evalmut/muteval). All are small and young, which means there is room for a better one, but not an unoccupied category.
- What I could not find a tool for: running a benchmark's own grader (Inspect scorer, Harbor verifier, SWE-bench harness) against a library of wrong/valid/nondeterminism fixtures as a CI check. Absence of evidence from my searches, not proof.
